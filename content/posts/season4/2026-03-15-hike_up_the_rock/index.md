Last year we somehow avoided hiking up to the top of the rock, but this time we resolved to do it!
Hiking from the town to the summit is 1,400 feet, but it seems much higher since the surrounding 
country is fairly flat.  Beyond just being a mountain, the Rock of Gibraltar has been used as fortified base to
control the entrance to the Mediterranean.  Since 1704, the British have managed the property (the Spanish are not especially happy with that)
and through the end of WWII had expanded the system of tunnels that have been dug into the rock.

We had heard that there is a troop of Barbary Macaques (the only wild monkeys in Europe) that live on the rock and before starting the hike we bought some apples
to feed them (we learned later you were not supposed to do that).  The store only sold apples by the bag, so I ended up hefting a heavy bag of fruit in my backpack.
