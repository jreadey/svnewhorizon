 Before we left, I had to go up the mast to free our main sail halyard -- it had gotten caught on a lazy line block high up
 the mast.  This is the first time I had gone up the mast at anchor, but the rocking due to the wind and waves wasn't too bad.

 We left Malta in the afternoon to immediately run into to a large regatta!  After maneuvering around various race boats,
 we set a full main and genoa -- the wind was around 15 kts, but predicted to become lighter.  Instead the winds kept up
 the entire night.  We were in danger of arriving in Siracusa at 3am, so I did a turn away from shore to 
 burn some time.  In the end we got into the harbour just as the sun was rising.  The harbour is rather large with just a few
 sailboats, so we had no problem finding an anchorage.

 It was Sunday, so we couldn't check into Italy (offices were closed), so we spent the rest of the day relaxing and getting 
 caught up on our sleep.
