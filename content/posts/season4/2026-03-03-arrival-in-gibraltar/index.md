We flew from Seattle to London to Gibraltar to meet up with New Horizon.  The boat was actually across the border in La Linea, Spain.  But it's a quick walk to get to the Spanish side.

One odd thing about the Gibraltar airport is that you need to cross the runway to get to Spain.  So periodically they will halt flights while people troop over 
to get to the border crossing.
