In Cassis, Jia and Xiao-Yi met up with us to stay on the boat for a couple of days before
continuing their French vacation.  The wind was blowing from the east, so we decided to sail
to Marseille which is just a few miles east of Cassis.  It was a fairly bumpy ride though and
our guests got a little more wave action than they were expecting.  

On arriving in Marseille rather than entering the busy harbour, we anchored in the small island 
just outside, Ile de Ratonneau.  The next day we dinghied ashore and explored the island.
After a last meal on board and a few rounds of Mah-jong, it was time for Jia and her husband to continue
their vacation, so we all took the island ferry into Marseille.  We said good-bye and then spent some
time at the museum and walking around the city.  Marseille has a reputation as a gritty town, but we found it
very pleasant!
