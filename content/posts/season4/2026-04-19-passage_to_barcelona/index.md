From Valencia our next destination was Barcelona, but we made a short stop in Ginesta (about 20 nm from Barcelona)
to meet a rigger who could complete the fix on our genoa foil.  Docking in Ginesta, med mooring (where the transom of the 
boat faces the dock and you pull up two underwater lines to secure the bow) was relatively new to the captain who tried to use
his bare hands to pull up the underwater (slime) line.  The barnacles on the line are really sharp and in no time he was bleeding all
over the deck!

Anyway, the rigger was able to fix the foil quickly and it wasn't even that expensive.  So that was a relief and we set out for the
short trip to Barcelona.
