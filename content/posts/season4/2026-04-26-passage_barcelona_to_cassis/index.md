We only stayed in Barcelona for a couple of days as we needed to get to France to meet up with Rong's sister and husband.
Still we got a chance to visit with Emily and see Barcelona again.  The neighborhood where our marina was is very active, with a lot
of beach goers, joggers, and cyclists.  It felt like every Barcelona resident was on the move.  

Emily toured us around and we enjoyed eating at several restaurants she picked out for us.  All too soon, it was time to 
leave for France.  The first day out there was hardly any wind as we skirted the Spanish coast line.  Next day we headed into open water
to cross the Gulf of Lyon headed towards Cassis (near Marseille).  The wind became quite strong and we scudded along with just a tightly reefed genoa.  

On the third day we arrived in Cassis.  Cassis is known for its *calanques* - narrow steep walled rocky inlets that are common along the 
French coastline near Cassis.  We attempted to catch a mooring ball at the calanque closest to Cassis, but were told our boat was too big!  We had no choice but to make our way to the marina in Cassis.
Cassis is a cute town and it is close enough to several of the calanques that we were able to hike there the next day.  Really tiring even though the horizontal distance was not great, there is a lot of up and down traveling!
