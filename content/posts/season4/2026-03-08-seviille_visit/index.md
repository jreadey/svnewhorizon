While in Gibraltar we wanted to visit some of the interesting nearby cities like Seville.  Unfortunately Seville is inland, so 
we couldn't sail there.  Instead we rented a car and drove two and half hours to Seville.
 
The drive wasn't bad.  In Seville I had booked a room at Torre Sevilla which is one of the few tall buildings in Seville. We had a nice view from our room, but 
the view from the atrium on the roof is amazing.
{{< figure src="IMG_3317.jpg" alt="atrium view" caption="Atrium view" >}}
