It was an easy dinghy ride/walk from our anchorage in Villefranche to the train station, so
we used the train to explore some of the nearby towns (or in the case of Monaco, countries!).

Monaco was our first trip; it was just a few minutes away by train, but an entirely different country!  Not that there 
are any actual border checks even though Monaco isn't part of the EU.
Everything in Monaco is very clean, tidy, and expensive.  We had a pricey coffee and pastry, then took a look
at the casino.  Not willing to risk money actually gambling, we then walked up the city until we got to the palace.
Luckily we got there just in time to see the changing of the guard which is a popular attraction.  We were a week early 
for the Grand Prix, but since we didn't have/couldn't afford tickets, it was probably better to come before the big crowds
of the Grand Prix arrived.

Our next outing was to Eze.  Eze is a cute little village perched high on a hilltop.  It's usually mobbed by tourists,
but is quite picturesque. 

Finally, we went to the town of Grasse, known as the perfume capital of France.  We are not big on perfume,
but we bought some fragrances to take back home as gifts.
