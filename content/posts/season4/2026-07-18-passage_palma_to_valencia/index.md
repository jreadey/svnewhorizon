We met up with Nai-Rong and Elaine in Palma.  We spent a couple of days touring Palma together and then 
it was time to set sail for Valencia.  Leaving the med moor in Palma once of the stanchions got hooked on the dock's slime line, so 
we got another bet stanchion (I'm going to need to start buying these in bulk).
Once we got off, the wind was light so we interspersed some slow speed sailing with
motoring as we made our way west.
Along the way we stopped for an afternoon in Evissa to take a break and enjoy some paddle boarding
and snorkeling.
