After some time relaxing in VilleFranche we decided it was time to see Corsica.
It's about a hundred miles sail and we had an easy over night passage arriving in Calvi Bay early morning.
We anchored in Calvi Bay which is nice and wide with good holding.  Which was good since the wind picked up and we ended up 
staying in Calvin for a week before it was calm enough to head south along the Corsica coast.
