After our day tour of Valletta, we took a hub-on hop-off bus to Mdina.  Mdina is located in the center of the island 
of Malta.  Before the 1800's Mdina was the capital of Malta and had been occupied since the bronze age.  By being far away (for Malta)
from the shore and with a good 360 degree view, it was well protected from attack by pirates or hostile powers.  Later the capital was 
moved to Valletta and Mdina became a quiet town, hence the name "the Silent City".  Now it is a very interesting place to
visit where it seems little changed from what it must have been in the middle ages.
