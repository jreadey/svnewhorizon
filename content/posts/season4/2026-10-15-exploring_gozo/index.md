Gozo is Malta's second largest island.  We didn't get a chance to stop there on our way in
to the main island, so we took a ferry to visit the island.  Gozo has one of the most interesting 
archaeological site of [Ggantija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%A0gantija).  Legend has the
the island was populated by a race of giants who assembled the large structures.  In reality, researchers 
are still working to understand how a stone age people created these sites.

Later in the day we had lunch in Victoria (the largest city in Gozo, and toured the Citadel, the fortification
overlooking the city.
