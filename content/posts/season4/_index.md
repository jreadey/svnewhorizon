Welcome to **Season 4**! 

This section covers leaving out winter base near Gibraltar and heading into the Mediterranean proper.  We sail along the Spainish and French costs, explore Corsica and the Balearic Islands before crossing to Tunisia for the winter season.  Finally, we have a late season sail from Tunisia to circumnavigate the isalnd of Sicily before putting New Horizon to bed for the winter.
